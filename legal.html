1. Terms of Service

67 Royale (“67”, “we”, “us”) is an online social and entertainment service where users can post, discuss, discover and compete around internet culture. By using 67, you agree to these Terms and the Community Rules.

Eligibility

For the initial launch, 67 is intended for people aged 16 or older. We may apply additional age restrictions where required by local law. Do not create an account if you are not eligible.

Your account

You are responsible for activity on your account, keeping access credentials secure, and providing accurate information. Do not impersonate another person, create accounts to evade restrictions, or automate activity in a way that manipulates rankings or platform systems.

Your content

You retain ownership of content you create. You grant 67 a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to host, reproduce, display, distribute and technically adapt that content only as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, moderate, promote and improve the service. You confirm that you have the rights needed to submit the content.

Platform rights

We may remove, restrict, label or limit content and accounts that violate these Terms, Community Rules, applicable law, or create material safety/security risk. Where appropriate, we will provide a reason and a route to appeal.

Availability

67 is provided on an “as available” basis. Features may change, be interrupted, or be discontinued. We do not guarantee uninterrupted service, perfect trend detection, or that every AI answer is accurate.

Third-party services

67 may connect to or reference services such as X, TikTok, YouTube, crypto/NFT data providers, hosting providers and AI providers. Those services have their own terms and privacy practices. 67 does not control third-party services.

Termination

You may stop using 67 at any time. We may suspend or terminate accounts where permitted by law and these Terms. We will not use termination powers to remove lawful criticism merely because it is critical of 67.

3. Community Rules

  • No illegal content, threats, targeted harassment, doxxing or credible encouragement of violence.
  • No sexual exploitation or sexual content involving minors.
  • No non-consensual intimate imagery.
  • No scams, phishing, malware, impersonation or coordinated fraud.
  • No spam, automated engagement, fake accounts, vote manipulation or ELO manipulation.
  • No content that unlawfully infringes another person’s intellectual property or privacy rights.
  • Crypto/NFT content must not be presented as an official 67 investment product or guarantee of returns.
  • Commercial promotions and sponsored content must be clearly identifiable where required by law.
  • Do not attempt to exploit, disrupt, probe or gain unauthorised access to 67 or another user’s account.

Context matters. We aim to enforce these rules proportionately and consistently, with human review available for significant moderation disputes where required.

4. Cookies & Tracking

67 will use strictly necessary storage for login/session functionality. Non-essential analytics, advertising or tracking technologies will only be activated where the required consent has been obtained. Users will be able to change or withdraw consent through the site's cookie controls.

The Irish Data Protection Commission states that non-exempt cookies/tracking generally require consent and that withdrawing consent should be as easy as giving it.

5. 67 AI

67 AI may summarise, classify, explain or generate content using automated systems and third-party AI providers. AI output can be wrong, incomplete, outdated or misleading. Users should verify important claims independently.

AI trend scores and explanations are not guarantees of popularity, authenticity, financial performance or future outcomes. Crypto and NFT answers are informational and are not financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

We will not represent an automated score as an objective fact. Where AI materially contributes to moderation or account decisions, the applicable review and appeal process will be available.

6. Royale, ELO & Royals

ELO is an in-platform competitive ranking. Royals are in-platform points used for progression or future cosmetic features. Unless expressly stated otherwise, Royals have no cash value, are not redeemable for money, are not a cryptocurrency, and are not transferable between users.

Royale battles are entertainment features. ELO outcomes may depend on votes and platform scoring systems. We may adjust scoring to maintain fair competition and prevent manipulation.

At launch, 67 will not sell Royale tickets or Pro subscriptions. Any future paid features will have separate, clearly presented purchase and cancellation terms before payment is requested.

7. Reports, Illegal Content & Appeals

Users should be able to report content they believe is illegal or violates the Community Rules through an accessible electronic reporting route. Reports should include enough information for us to identify the relevant content and reason for the report.

Where we take a moderation or account action that is appealable under applicable law, we will provide information about the decision and a way to request review. We may use automated tools for triage, but significant decisions will be subject to appropriate human oversight where required.
